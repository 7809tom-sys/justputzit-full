## Publication Standard

The member-facing feed must contain only current, individually dated events with an official handoff, or public-access venue activities whose operating information is explicitly sourced. “Locally owned” labels require first-party evidence from the business. City names and venues must be stored exactly as stated by the source; regional calendars are not enough to label an activity as a city-local listing.

## Needed Activity Coverage

The statewide taxonomy should explicitly include public coffeehouses and dining, book clubs and library programs, arcade and game venues, live music and comedy, ticketed or cover-charge entertainment, student-facing recreation, outdoor activities, community groups, markets, theatre, and adult/family programming.
