# Official Church and Lions Club Source Evidence

**Scope.** This rollout adds only public, official, dated sources within the current Milwaukee/Waukesha local-discovery footprint. It does not assert coverage of every congregation or Lions Club. A source is eligible only when it is operated by the organization itself, exposes future public activities, keeps a city-precise location or city boundary, and provides a direct official handoff. Member logins, contact details, and internal meetings are deliberately excluded.
